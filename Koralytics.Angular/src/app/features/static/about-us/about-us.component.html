Market Standard Telemetry

Establishing Regional Performance Standards

Koralytics leverages aggregated, non-sensitive football statistics (such as pass completion ratios, sprint velocities by age category, and drill completion speeds) to build unified regional standards.

This enables academies to measure their teams and players against regional market averages without ever exposing sensitive private operational data.
