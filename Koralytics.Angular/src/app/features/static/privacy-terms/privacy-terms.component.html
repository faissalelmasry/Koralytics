Data Privacy & Terms of Service.

Our explicit commitment to minor protection, 100% academy data ownership, and strict PII anonymization firewalls.

Child Safety Standard

1. Minor Privacy Protection Policy

Koralytics enforces strict regulatory compliance policies governing all data collection, storage, and processing for young athletes and children under 18.

Parental Consent First

Minor athlete profiles can only be created and managed under authorized academy administration with parent/guardian portal verification.

Encrypted Identity & Contact Details

Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as home addresses, phone numbers, and school details are strictly encrypted and hidden from non-authorized users.

Scouter Inquiry Safeguards

Scouters and external talent queries can view sports performance radar metrics only. Direct communication with minor athletes is completely disabled; inquiries route directly to parents and head coaches.

100% Retained Rights

2. Academy Data Ownership Policy

Every sports academy, club, and organization using Koralytics retains 100% absolute legal ownership of their operational, financial, and athlete data.

No Third-Party Monetization

Koralytics never sells, leases, or trades private academy data, subscription histories, or contact records to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Data Export & Portability

Academy managers can export complete squad telemetry, drill records, and financial reports in structured JSON or CSV format at any time without penalty.

Instant Deletion Rights

Upon contract termination, all private academy databases and isolated tenant tables are purged permanently within 30 days in compliance with deletion SLAs.

By registering an academy account or accessing Koralytics portals, you agree to comply with our platform terms of service.
